Delete your Evide School Bus account

If you use the Evide School Bus parent app, you can ask us to delete your account and the personal data linked to it. This page explains how, and what happens to your data.

How to request deletion

  1. Send your requestFill in the form below using the email address you sign in to the app with. You can also email us instead.
  2. We confirm it's youWe reply to your registered email to confirm the request, so no one else can delete your account.
  3. We delete your dataYour account and personal data are deleted within 30 days of confirmation, and we email you when it's done.

Request account deletion

Prefer email? Write to irfan@evide.in from your registered email address with the subject “Delete my Evide School Bus account”.

What we delete

DataWhat happens
Your parent accountDeleted: your name, email, phone number and login details.
Your link to your childDeleted: your account is removed from your child's record, so you can no longer see the bus.
Notifications and device dataDeleted: notification history sent to you and the device token used to send you alerts.
Pickup and drop location for your childDeleted from your account. If your school still needs it to run the bus, the school keeps its own copy.

What we keep, and for how long

Some information belongs to your child's school rather than to your app account. Your child's student record, bus assignment and the bus's own GPS history are managed by the school, and are not deleted when you delete your app account. To have your child's student record removed, please contact the school directly.

Backup copies of deleted data are overwritten automatically within 30 days of deletion. We only keep information longer where the law requires us to, and only for as long as it requires.

Questions

Email irfan@evide.in. We usually reply within 2 working days.
